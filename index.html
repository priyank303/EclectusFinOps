We act as an extension of your finance department — working alongside your CFO and functional leaders to adapt your existing architecture. We ensure that your P2P and O2C data feeds seamlessly into your accounting systems, equipping your team to handle regulatory shifts without the anxiety of operational disruption.

Enterprise Innovation · Amazon Case Study

Project Simba:
From Monolithic Code to a Configurable Engine

At Amazon, Priyank architected the transition from legacy, hardcoded monolithic finance systems to Simba — a modular, configurable engine serving both Accounts Payable (P2P) and Accounts Receivable (O2C).

Legacy Monolithic Approach

Hardcoded Logic & Fragile Deployments

Tax rates, invoice validation, and accounting treatment were embedded directly inside monolithic backend code. Every statutory update required engineering cycles and extensive regression testing.

  • 3–6 Month Release Cycles: Code changes gated behind quarterly sprint freezes
  • Fragmented Logic: Payables (P2P) and Receivables (O2C) operated on divergent rule sets
  • VAT Leakage Risk: Delayed rollouts caused retroactive tax remediation and penalties
Simba Configurable Platform

Policy-Driven Configurable Architecture

Decoupled tax rules, statutory mandates, and invoice matching logic from core code into a high-performance, configurable policy engine across 80+ jurisdictions.

  • Near Instantaneous Updates (< 60s): Finance teams configure rules without code deployments
  • Unified P2P & O2C Synchronization: Single source of truth for all supplier and customer tax flows
  • Point-of-Injection Validation: Over $50M in historical VAT leakage eradicated
